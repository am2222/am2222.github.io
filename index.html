What I work on

I work on geospatial AI and build open-source tools for spatial data.

  • 🤖 Agentic AI: production multi-agent systems with LangChain/LangGraph, MCP servers and A2A/AG-UI, evaluated with Langfuse
  • 🛰️ 3D reconstruction: drone imagery → COLMAP → Triangle Splatting+ on an NVIDIA GB10 → Cesium 3D Tiles
  • 🌐 DGGS: Discrete Global Grid Systems in the browser, in DuckDB and in Python, and as a representation for spatial learning
  • 📄 Research: 600+ citations, including LLMs and spatial concepts (COSIT 2024), DGGS as a Digital Earth, and decentralized geoprivacy
